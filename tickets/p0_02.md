## Scope

`compose.yaml` arm64: Postgres+Timescale, Redis, MinIO, migrate, api, worker(dummy), web; `PROVISIONER_MODE=static`, `ACTION_DRY_RUN=true`

## Technical Notes

Target utama **lokal Mac M2 16 GB**: orchestration = **docker-compose (bukan K8s)**. Set `PROVISIONER_MODE=static` (BE tak panggil K8s API), `ACTION_DRY_RUN=true` default, `ACTION_BATCH_PARALLELISM=2`. Pin image **multi-arch/arm64** (Playwright `*-noble`, timescale, minio, redis) dan sediakan `--scale worker=3` (maks 3 di M2). Lihat `SYSTEM_DESIGN.md` §Local Tier & `INFRA_ANALYST.md` §15.2.

Utamakan YAGNI — hanya bangun yang dipakai P1. Semua service: non-root, read-only rootfs, drop capabilities, HEALTHCHECK, resource request+limit. **Lingkungan build = lokal Mac M2 16 GB (docker-compose, ARM64, tanpa K8s)** — lihat `INFRA_ANALYST.md` §15.1/§15.2.
